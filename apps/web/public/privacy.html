2. The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your game is then stored only in your browser on this device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email, a hashed password and your game data so your save follows you across devices.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics are off by default. They are only sent if you turn them on in Settings → Region & Privacy.
  • No advertising, no tracking cookies, no analytics services, no selling of data.
  • You can download or delete all your account data at any time in Settings → Account.
