## 1. The Historical Context: The Twin Crises of Our Century

As we design *District: Common Ground* to remain relevant and living over the next decade (2026–2036+), we must recognize the defining systemic feedback loop of modern urban history:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              THE CLIMATE DISPLACEMENT & FASCISM FEEDBACK LOOP           │
│                                                                         │
│  [GLOBAL HEATING & ECOLOGICAL SHOCKS]                                   │
│  • Heat domes, agricultural failure, drought, flash floods              │
│  • Displacement of rural & coastal populations across borders          │
│                      │                                                  │
│                      ▼                                                  │
│  [URBAN INFLUX & HOUSING/RESOURCE SQUEEZE]                              │
│  • Working-class districts experience sudden population growth          │
│  • Infrastructure strained by decades of public disinvestment           │
│                      │                                                  │
│         ┌────────────┴────────────┐                                     │
│         ▼                         ▼                                     │
│  [FAR-RIGHT REACTION]     [SOLIDARITY COMMONS]                         │
│  • Scapegoat the migrant  • Welcome new hands & skills                  │
│  • Far-right disinformation• Expand Community Kitchen & Land Trust      │
│  • Border-policing, raids • Sanctuary Defense & Mutual Care             │
│  • Desolation & Fascism   • Multiracial Resilient Flourishing           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

When climate shocks displace human beings, far-right propaganda apparatuses and predatory opportunists weaponize the resulting resource scarcity. They redirect popular frustration away from the corporate polluters, speculative landlords, and austerity budgets—and toward the most vulnerable newcomer.

In *District: Common Ground*, **welcoming climate migrants is not charity—it is the vital engine of community survival**. New arrivals bring construction skills, agricultural knowledge, childcare capacity, and cultural vitality that expand the neighborhood's capacity to survive future climate shocks.

## 2. Core Systemic Mechanics

### 2.1 The "Welcoming Commons" Mechanic (Climate Displacement Influx)
Throughout the year, climate alert events (e.g. extreme summer heatwaves, regional flood evacuations) trigger newcomer arrivals at the North Transit Station.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      MIGRANT INTEGRATION DYNAMICS                      │
├────────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CLOSED DISTRICT (Scapegoating) │ SANCTUARY DISTRICT (Solidarity)       │
├────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • Report undocumented arrivals │ • Provide emergency shelter in Church │
│ • Harass street vendors        │   or Community Hall                   │
│ • Install anti-homeless benches│ • Share food at the Community Fridge  │
│                                │ • Issue municipal community ID badges │
├────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────┤
│ CONSEQUENCE:                   │ CONSEQUENCE:                          │
│ • Short-term budget relief     │ • Initial energy/food strain (-15)    │
│ • Police militarization        │ • Labor capacity surges by +30%       │
│ • Long-term population decline │ • Unlocks: Rooftop Agro-Greenhouse,   │
│ • Vulnerability to next shock  │   Bilingual Care Network, Solar Team  │
│ • World desaturates to gray    │ • World blooms into cultural festival │
└────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **The Demographic Dividend**: When newcomers are welcomed, the district's construction and repair throughput increases. Tasks that previously required Arthur or Morgan now have a growing volunteer cooperative.
- **Cultural Enrichments**: New languages, food recipes at the Community Kitchen, street music traditions, and architectural shade styles appear across the district.

### 2.2 The Far-Right Propaganda & Disinformation Defense Engine
Far-right groups, algorithmic clickbait platforms, and speculative real estate bots actively target distressed districts with stochastic propaganda:

1. **The Disinformation Vector**:
   - Leaflets slipped under tenement doors: *"Migrants taking free hotel rooms while locals freeze!"*
   - Viral social video hoaxes: AI-generated video claiming the cultural center is a smuggling front.
   - Far-right demagogues holding "town halls" in nearby private venues to agitate for neighborhood patrols.
2. **Community Counter-Measures (Player Action)**:
   - **Tear Down Hate Propaganda**: Walk through alleys to rip down agitator flyers (`+5 Trust, -2 Energy`).
   - **Open Doors & Sunlight**: Host an open neighborhood potluck at the cultural center to disprove rumors with lived reality.
   - **Fact-Checking Broadsheet**: Work with Sal at the grocer to print fact-checks and community statements in *"The Daily District Ground"*.
   - **Sanctuary Whistle Network**: Create an emergency phone/whistle tree that peacefully swarms the street if immigration or vigilante raids occur, filming and de-escalating.

To ensure the game lives, adapts, and teaches systems thinking over the next decade, the backend ingestion pipeline is designed with **climate-trajectory awareness**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   THE 10-YEAR OPEN-DATA HORIZON                        │
│                                                                        │
│  [GLOBAL TRAJECTORY INGESTION: apps/api/internal/pulse/climate.go]     │
│  • NOAA / NASA GISTEMP: Global Surface Temperature Anomaly             │
│  • Extreme Heat Days Index (Regional Heat Dome Tracking)               │
│  • UNHCR / IDMC Global Internal Displacement Monitoring Data           │
│  • ACLED (Armed Conflict & Civic Protest Real-time Monitoring)         │
│                                                                        │
│  [SCALING DISTRICT ENVIRONMENT (2026 ──> 2036)]                        │
│  • Ambient temperature baselines adjust realistically year over year   │
│  • Summer heatwaves increase in frequency and intensity                │
│  • Migrant arrivals scale naturally with real-world displacement data  │
│  • The game remains an honest, living mirror of the planet's state     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Long-Term Planetary Ingestion Feeds
- **NOAA / Copernicus Climate Change Service**:
  - Automatically raises ambient summer temperatures and heat-island effects in the game.
  - Generates realistic "Wet-Bulb Temperature Crises" where un-airconditioned apartments become deadly unless the Community Cooling Center is activated.
- **UNHCR Open Data**:
  - Scales the frequency of newcomer families arriving at the train station based on real global displaced population benchmarks (currently over 114 million globally).
- **Civil Society & Hate-Watch Observatories**:
  - Tracks spikes in regional xenophobic political legislation and scapegoating media rhetoric, dynamically spawning relevant counter-disinformation story arcs.

## 4. Specific Crisis Scenarios: Racism, Disinformation & Climate Shock

Here are four new foundational scenarios designed for `crisis_scenarios.json` and the news generator:

### Scenario 6: "The Heat Dome & The Cooling Sanctuary"
- **Context**: A record $41^\circ\text{C}$ ($106^\circ\text{F}$) heat dome strikes the concrete district. The private commercial mall locks its doors to "non-customers" and guards the air conditioning. Elderly residents and unhoused migrants on the street face fatal heat stroke.
- **Scapegoat Choice**: *Prioritize power for private businesses; tell street folks to seek shelter in the industrial zone.* (+$25 Cash, -20 Trust, -15 Resilience, 4 heat casualties).
- **Solidarity Choice**: *Occupy the shaded Town Hall, activate the solar battery backup, and distribute free cold water and electrolyte stew.* (-20 Energy, +25 Trust, +20 Resilience; builds permanent community cooling protocol).

### Scenario 7: "The Bot-Farm Smear Campaign"
- **Context**: An astroturfed social media group floods neighborhood message boards with claims that newly arrived asylum seekers caused a recent string of burglaries. In reality, a corporate developer hired actors to destabilize the block so residents sell cheaply.
- **Scapegoat Choice**: *Form an armed civilian neighborhood watch that stops and questions brown and immigrant pedestrians.* (+$40 Cash from landlord defense fund, -30 Trust, -25 Resilience, Police martial state triggered).
- **Solidarity Choice**: *Organize a community investigative committee. Trace the bot accounts back to the developer's PR agency, host a street rally, and establish a cross-cultural buddy walk system.* (-15 Energy, -$10 Cash, +30 Trust, +25 Resilience, World blooms).

### Scenario 8: "The Undocumented Construction Collective"
- **Context**: City inspectors threaten to halt the rooftop solar installation, claiming the volunteer crew includes undocumented workers lacking union certifications.
- **Scapegoat Choice**: *Dismiss the undocumented workers immediately to appease city hall.* (-10 Trust, -20 Resilience; solar project stalls for 3 weeks).
- **Solidarity Choice**: *Partner with the local progressive labor union to issue apprentice cards, declare the solar site a community sanctuary zone, and picket city hall until permits are granted.* (-20 Energy, +25 Trust, +30 Resilience; unlocks advanced Solar Engineering buff).

### Scenario 9: "The Far-Right Agitator Rally"
- **Context**: A far-right extremist group from out of town announces a "Save Our Streets" anti-migrant march through your Central Plaza this Saturday, accompanied by provocative online threats.
- **Scapegoat Choice**: *Close all shutters, stay indoors, and let them march unopposed.* (-15 Trust, -20 Resilience, fear paralyzes the neighborhood).
- **Solidarity Choice**: *Organize a massive, peaceful "Festival of Common Ground"—bring out street brass bands, dancing, free food, and human chains protecting the mosque, synagogue, and cultural center.* (-25 Energy, -$15 Cash, +35 Trust, +35 Resilience; far-right marchers are drowned out and leave demoralized).

## 5. Architectural & Visual Representation

- **Inclusive Visual Design**:
  - Visual diversity across ethnicities, religious attire (hijabs, turbans, kippahs), ages, physical abilities (wheelchair ramps at the Community Kitchen), and gender presentations.
  - Multilingual street signage: Murals and signs in Spanish, Arabic, Vietnamese, and English reflecting real immigrant neighborhoods.
- **Audio Soundscapes of Resistance**:
  - Procedural Web Audio synth expands to include rhythmic street clapping, brass horn fanfare, multilingual ambient cafe murmur, and megaphone chants of solidarity during rallies.
